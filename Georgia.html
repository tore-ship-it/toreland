Quick Facts • Capital and Largest City: Atlanta • Population: ~11.3 million (8th most populous U.S. state) • Statehood Date: January 2, 1788 (4th state to join the Union) • Nicknames: The Peach State, Empire State of the South • Motto: "Wisdom, Justice & Moderation" • Borders: Tennessee and North Carolina to the north, South Carolina and the Atlantic Ocean to the east, Florida to the south, and Alabama to the west. Geography & Economy • Landforms: Features the Blue Ridge Mountains in the north, rolling Piedmont hills in the center, and coastal plains/barrier islands along the Atlantic Ocean. • Key Hubs: Home to Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport (one of the world's busiest airports) and the Port of Savannah. • Major Industries: Logistics, filmmaking, technology, manufacturing, and agriculture (leading in poultry and peanuts).
